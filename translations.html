《俱舍英译百瑕》：英译本一百处错误One Hundred Flaws in the English Translations of the Kośa

Wang Lu read the two English translations of the Abhidharmakośabhāṣya against Xuanzang's Chinese and recorded one hundred errors, mostly in chapters 5–8 (the anuśaya, path, knowledge and meditation chapters). Each entry quotes the English, gives Xuanzang's text (and Paramārtha's or the Sanskrit where useful), and explains the doctrinal point. Errors may originate in La Vallée Poussin's French or in the Sanskrit; the book judges only on doctrinal grounds.王路以玄奘译本对读《俱舍》两部英译本，记下一百处错误，主要在后四品（随眠、贤圣、智、定）。每条列出英译与玄奘译文（必要时兼列真谛本或梵本），并从阿毗达磨义理上说明。错误有的来自 LVP 法译或梵本，书中只就义理指误。

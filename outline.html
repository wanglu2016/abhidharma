说明Notes

一、 阿毗达磨思想是流变的。本书既然是基础教程，一般不介绍流变。对存在不同见解的问题，一般只给出某个时期的主流见解，通常是世亲菩萨、众贤论师时期的有部主流见解。

1. Abhidharma thought evolved over time. Since this is a basic course, it generally does not describe that evolution. Where views differ, it usually gives only the mainstream view of one period, normally the mainstream Sarvāstivāda view of the time of Vasubandhu and Saṃghabhadra.

二、 由于阿毗达磨的系统性，在讲解一个概念和观点时，会经常用到其他概念和观点，所以，阅读本书前面的章节时如果有不理解的地方，应该暂时跳过，学习完后面的章节会对前面的章节有更深入的理解。

2. Because Abhidharma is systematic, explaining one concept or position often requires others. If something in an earlier chapter is unclear, skip it for now; after studying the later chapters you will understand the earlier ones more deeply.

三、 在学习基础教程时，不适合多问“为什么”，而适合多记住“是什么”。“为什么”的问题是应该在学完高阶教程《俱舍九题》之后才适合问的。

3. At the basic stage it is better not to ask "why" too often, but to remember "what". Questions of "why" are best asked after finishing the advanced course, the Nine Questions.

四、 本书面对的主要读者是没有熟练背会《俱舍论颂》的人。书中的“颂曰”指的是《俱舍论颂》。《俱舍论颂》包含的定义和观点，本书会优先引用。

4. The main readers of this book are those who have not yet memorised the Verses of the Abhidharmakośa. "The verse says" (頌曰) refers to the Verses of the Kośa. Definitions and positions contained in the Verses are cited first.

五、 阿毗达磨有它的术语体系。本书对术语的解释，不以梵语语法为标准，也不以汉语语法为标准，唯以阿毗达磨义理为标准。

5. Abhidharma has its own system of terms. Terms are explained here neither by Sanskrit grammar nor by Chinese grammar, but solely by Abhidharma doctrine.

六、 阿毗达磨的见解和经部、唯识存在很多不同，学习本书时，请不要受其他学说的影响。

6. Abhidharma views differ in many respects from those of the Sautrāntikas and of Yogācāra. While studying this book, do not let other doctrines influence you.

第1章Chapter 1

1.1一切法、四谛

一切法：有为、无为。

All dharmas: conditioned (有为 saṃskṛta) and unconditioned (无为 asaṃskṛta).

四相：生、住、异、灭。

The four characteristics: arising (生), duration (住), change (异), cessation (灭).

有为法和无为法的区别在于，有为法具有四相：生、住、异、灭。无为法只具有一相：住。因此，有个表达叫“有为三相”：生、异、灭。假如用“生住异灭”表达“有为三相”，有部把它解释为：生、住异、灭。其中，“住异”就是“异”相。这是因为契经常说“有为三相”，有时又说“生住异灭”。“住相”通有为无为法，在说“有为三相”时略去不说。

Conditioned and unconditioned dharmas differ in that conditioned dharmas have the four characteristics of arising, duration, change and cessation, whereas unconditioned dharmas have only one: duration. Hence the expression "the three characteristics of the conditioned": arising, change, cessation. When "arising, duration, change, cessation" is used to express the three characteristics of the conditioned, the Sarvāstivāda reads it as: arising, duration-change, cessation, where "duration-change" is simply the characteristic of change. This is because the sūtras usually speak of "three characteristics of the conditioned" but sometimes say "arising, duration, change, cessation". Duration is common to conditioned and unconditioned dharmas, so it is left unmentioned when the three characteristics of the conditioned are stated.

一切法：有漏、无漏。

All dharmas: with-outflow (有漏 sāsrava) and outflow-free (无漏 anāsrava).

有漏：颂曰：“于彼漏随增，故说名有漏。”

With-outflow: the verse says, "Because the outflows adhere-and-grow in them, they are called with-outflow."

漏是烦恼的多种异名之一。漏如果于一法随增，则说此法“有漏”。什么叫“于一法随增”“于彼随增”呢？“于彼”是“由于彼”的意思，也可以按汉语字面理解为“在它上”。如果漏因为某法而随逐增长，则说某法有漏。

Outflow (漏 āsrava) is one of the many synonyms for defilement. If the outflows adhere-and-grow (随增) with respect to a dharma, that dharma is said to be with-outflow. What does "adhere-and-grow with respect to a dharma" (于彼随增) mean? 于彼 means "by reason of it"; it can also be taken literally as "on it". If the outflows follow along and grow because of a certain dharma, that dharma is with-outflow.

随增：所缘随增、相应随增。

Adhering-and-growing (随增 anuśayana): by way of the object (所缘随增) and by way of association (相应随增).

所缘随增，是说漏缘某法，某法作为漏的所缘，令漏增长。相应随增，是说漏与某法相应，某法作为漏的相应法，令漏增长。无论所缘随增还是相应随增，都要求此法未断。如果此法已断，漏不会于彼随增。

Adhering-and-growing by way of the object: an outflow takes a dharma as its object, and that dharma, as the object of the outflow, makes it grow. Adhering-and-growing by way of association: an outflow is associated with a dharma, and that dharma, as a dharma associated with the outflow, makes it grow. In either case the dharma must not yet be abandoned. If it has been abandoned, the outflows do not adhere-and-grow with respect to it.

四谛：苦谛、集谛、灭谛、道谛。

The four truths: the truth of suffering (苦谛), the truth of origin (集谛), the truth of cessation (灭谛), the truth of the path (道谛).

四谛的总和不是一切法。一切法在四谛之外还包括：虚空、非择灭。

The four truths together are not all dharmas. Besides the four truths, all dharmas also include space (虚空) and cessation not through deliberation (非择灭).

有为法：苦谛、集谛、道谛。

Conditioned dharmas: the truths of suffering, origin and path.

无为法：灭谛、虚空、非择灭。

Unconditioned dharmas: the truth of cessation, space, cessation not through deliberation.

灭谛：择灭。

The truth of cessation: cessation through deliberation (择灭 pratisaṃkhyānirodha).

有漏法：苦谛、集谛。

With-outflow dharmas: the truths of suffering and origin.

无漏法：道谛、无为法。

Outflow-free dharmas: the truth of the path and the unconditioned dharmas.

无漏法：道谛、灭谛、虚空、非择灭。

Outflow-free dharmas: the truths of path and cessation, space, cessation not through deliberation.

三无为：择灭、非择灭、虚空。

The three unconditioned: cessation through deliberation, cessation not through deliberation, space.

一切有漏法都是苦谛，一切有漏法都是集谛。苦谛和集谛体同，行相不同。

Every with-outflow dharma is the truth of suffering, and every with-outflow dharma is the truth of origin. The truths of suffering and origin are the same in substance but differ in aspect (行相 ākāra).

苦谛四行相：非常、苦、空、非我。

The four aspects of the truth of suffering: impermanent, suffering, empty, non-self.

集谛四行相：因、集、生、缘。

The four aspects of the truth of origin: cause, origination, production, condition.

灭谛四行相：灭、静、妙、离。

The four aspects of the truth of cessation: cessation, calm, excellence, escape.

道谛四行相：道、如、行、出。

The four aspects of the truth of the path: path, suitability, practice, emancipation.

以上是“四谛十六行相”。行相是慧。慧是大地心所之一。十六行相有有漏，有无漏。顺决择分所起十六行相是有漏。

These are the "sixteen aspects of the four truths". An aspect is understanding (慧 prajñā), and understanding is one of the universal mental factors (大地法). The sixteen aspects can be with-outflow or outflow-free; those arising in the aids to penetration (顺决择分) are with-outflow.

四有：生有、本有、死有、中有。

The four existences (四有): birth-existence (生有), the existence of the time between (本有), death-existence (死有), intermediate existence (中有).

生有是受生的一刹那，本有是生有之后死有之前的刹那，死有是命终的一刹那，中有是死有之后生有之前的刹那。生有唯染污，余通善染无覆无记。

Birth-existence is the moment of taking rebirth; the existence of the time between covers the moments after birth-existence and before death-existence; death-existence is the moment of death; intermediate existence covers the moments after death-existence and before birth-existence. Birth-existence is only defiled; the others may be wholesome, defiled, or unobscured-indeterminate.

欲色界生有之前是中有。无色界生有之前是死有。无色界没有中有。中有是五蕴。颂曰：“死生二有中，五蕴名中有。未至应至处，故中有非生。”中有不称为“生”，而称为“起”。无色界没有色蕴。

In the sensuous and fine-material realms, birth-existence is preceded by intermediate existence. In the immaterial realm, birth-existence is preceded by death-existence: the immaterial realm has no intermediate existence. Intermediate existence consists of the five aggregates. The verse says: "Between the two existences of death and birth, the five aggregates are called intermediate existence. Because it has not yet reached the place it is to reach, intermediate existence is not birth." Intermediate existence is not called "birth" (生) but "arising" (起). The immaterial realm has no aggregate of matter.

1.2五蕴、十二处、十八界

五蕴：色蕴、受蕴、想蕴、行蕴、识蕴。

The five aggregates: matter (色蕴), feeling (受蕴), ideation (想蕴), formations (行蕴), consciousness (识蕴).

十二处：眼处、耳处、鼻处、舌处、身处、意处、色处、声处、香处、味处、触处、法处。

The twelve sense-bases: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body and mind bases; visible-form, sound, odour, taste, tangible and dharma bases.

十八界：眼界、耳界、鼻界、舌界、身界、意界、色界、声界、香界、味界、触界、法界、眼识界、耳识界、鼻识界、舌识界、身识界、意识界。

The eighteen elements: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body and mind elements; visible-form, sound, odour, taste, tangible and dharma elements; eye-, ear-, nose-, tongue-, body- and mind-consciousness elements.

一切法：五蕴、无为法。

All dharmas: the five aggregates and the unconditioned dharmas.

一切法：十二处。

All dharmas: the twelve sense-bases.

一切法：十八界。

All dharmas: the eighteen elements.

色蕴：五根、五境、无表色。

The aggregate of matter: the five sense faculties, the five objects, and non-informative matter (无表色).

五根：眼耳鼻舌身。

The five sense faculties: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body.

五境：色声香味触。

The five objects: visible form, sound, odour, taste, tangibles.

五根和五境，就是十二处中的十处，十八界中的十界，也叫“十色界”。在十色界之外，法处（法界）中的无表色也是色蕴。受蕴、想蕴是心所法，也是心所法中的大地法。

The five faculties and five objects are ten of the twelve sense-bases and ten of the eighteen elements, also called "the ten material elements". Beyond these ten, the non-informative matter within the dharma base (dharma element) is also part of the aggregate of matter. The aggregates of feeling and ideation are mental factors, specifically universal mental factors.

行蕴：心相应、心不相应。

The aggregate of formations: [formations] associated with mind (心相应) and dissociated from mind (心不相应).

相应的含义有五种：所依、所缘、行相、时、事平等；即所依、所缘、行相、时都相同，事平等是说每一刹那互为相应法的心和每种心所都只有一个。

Association (相应) has five senses: sameness of support, object, aspect, time and entity. That is, support, object, aspect and time are the same; "sameness of entity" means that in each moment the mind and each mental factor associated with one another occur only once.

识蕴：眼识、耳识、鼻识、舌识、身识、意识。

The aggregate of consciousness: eye-, ear-, nose-, tongue-, body- and mind-consciousness.

识蕴：意处。

The aggregate of consciousness: the mind base (意处).

识蕴：意界。

The aggregate of consciousness: the mind element (意界).

识蕴就是六识，六识也叫意处。意处和意界是相等的，范围大于意识，还包括眼等五识。意处也叫“七心界”。十八界中的七界是心，是识蕴，是意处，是意界。

The aggregate of consciousness is the six consciousnesses, which are also called the mind base. The mind base and the mind element are coextensive and broader than mind-consciousness, since they also include the five sense consciousnesses. The mind base is also called "the seven mind-elements". Seven of the eighteen elements are mind: they are the aggregate of consciousness, the mind base, the mind element.

1.3七十五法

五位75：色法、心法、心所法、心不相应行法、无为法。

The five groups, 75 [dharmas]: matter, mind, mental factors, conditionings dissociated from mind, unconditioned dharmas.

色法11：十色界、无表色。

Matter, 11: the ten material elements and non-informative matter.

心法1：识蕴。

Mind, 1: the aggregate of consciousness.

心所法46：大地法10、大善地法10、大烦恼地法6、大不善地法2、小烦恼地法10、不定地法8。

Mental factors, 46: universal 10, universal wholesome 10, universal defiled 6, universal unwholesome 2, minor defilements 10, indeterminate 8.

心不相应行法14：得、非得、同分、无想天、无想定、灭尽定、命根、生、住、异、灭、名身、句身、文身。

Dissociated conditionings, 14: acquisition (得), non-acquisition (非得), group-homogeneity (同分), the state of non-ideation (无想天), the attainment of non-ideation (无想定), the attainment of cessation (灭尽定), the vital faculty (命根), arising, duration, change, cessation, word-sets (名身), phrase-sets (句身), syllable-sets (文身).

无为法3：择灭、非择灭、虚空。

Unconditioned, 3: cessation through deliberation, cessation not through deliberation, space.

识蕴虽然包括六识，但在75 法中算成1 法。一相续中诸识不能俱起，一刹那只能生起一心。如果入无心定，则不生起心心所。

Although the aggregate of consciousness includes six consciousnesses, it counts as one dharma among the 75. In one continuum the consciousnesses cannot arise together: only one mind arises in a moment. In the mindless attainments, mind and mental factors do not arise.

无心定：无想定、灭尽定。

The mindless attainments (无心定): the attainment of non-ideation and the attainment of cessation.

“无想天”是无想异熟，是无覆无记性。二无心定是善性。

The "state of non-ideation" is the retribution of non-ideation and is unobscured-indeterminate. The two mindless attainments are wholesome.

大地法10：受、想、思、触、欲、慧、念、作意、胜解、三摩地。

Universal mental factors (大地法), 10: feeling, ideation, volition, contact, desire-to-act, understanding, mindfulness, attention, resolve, concentration.

大善地法10：信、不放逸、轻安、行舍、惭、愧、无贪、无瞋、不害、勤。

Universal wholesome factors (大善地法), 10: faith, heedfulness, pliancy, equanimity, shame, embarrassment, non-greed, non-hatred, non-harming, vigour.

大烦恼地法6：痴、放逸、懈怠、不信、昏沉、掉举。

Universal defiled factors (大烦恼地法), 6: delusion, heedlessness, indolence, faithlessness, torpor, restlessness.

大不善地法2：无惭、无愧。

Universal unwholesome factors (大不善地法), 2: shamelessness, non-embarrassment.

小烦恼地法10：忿、覆、悭、嫉、恼、害、恨、谄、诳、憍。

Minor defilements (小烦恼地法), 10: anger, concealment, avarice, envy, vexation, harmfulness, enmity, deceit, dissimulation, arrogance.

不定地法8：贪、瞋、慢、疑、恶作（悔）、睡眠、寻、伺。

Indeterminate factors (不定地法), 8: greed, hostility, conceit, doubt, remorse (恶作, also 悔), sleep, vitarka (寻), vicāra (伺).

2.1三界十五部

三界：欲界、色界、无色界。

The three realms: the sensuous realm (欲界), the fine-material realm (色界), the immaterial realm (无色界).

“三界”中的“色界”不同于“十八界”中的“色界”。前者和欲、无色界并列；后者和眼、耳、声等界并列。

The 色界 of the "three realms" differs from the 色界 of the "eighteen elements". The former stands alongside the sensuous and immaterial realms; the latter stands alongside the eye, ear, sound and other elements [and means the visible-form element].

五部：见苦所断、见集所断、见灭所断、见道所断、修所断。

The five classes (五部): abandoned by seeing suffering, by seeing origin, by seeing cessation, by seeing the path, and by cultivation.

十五部：欲界五部、色界五部、无色界五部。

The fifteen classes: the five classes of the sensuous realm, of the fine-material realm and of the immaterial realm.

十六部：十五部、非所断。

The sixteen classes: the fifteen classes plus the not-to-be-abandoned.

非所断：无漏。

Not to be abandoned (非所断): the outflow-free.

无漏法不系界地，但可以在色无色界八地生起，不在欲界生起。

Outflow-free dharmas are not bound to any realm or stage, but they can arise in the eight stages of the fine-material and immaterial realms; they do not arise in the sensuous realm.

九地：欲界、色界四静虑、无色界四定。

The nine stages (九地): the sensuous realm, the four dhyānas of the fine-material realm, the four [immaterial] attainments of the immaterial realm.

三地：有寻有伺地、无寻唯伺地、无寻无伺地。

The three stages (三地): with vitarka and vicāra, without vitarka but with vicāra only, without vitarka and without vicāra.

有寻有伺地：欲界、未至定、初静虑。

Stages with vitarka and vicāra: the sensuous realm, the not-yet-arrived concentration (未至定), the first dhyāna.

无寻唯伺地：静虑中间。

Stage without vitarka, with vicāra only: the intermediate dhyāna (静虑中间).

无寻无伺地：第二静虑乃至有顶及诸近分。

Stages without vitarka and vicāra: from the second dhyāna up to the summit of existence, together with the preparatory stages (近分).

无色界四定：空无边处、识无边处、无所有处、非想非非想处（有顶）。

The four immaterial attainments: the sphere of infinite space, of infinite consciousness, of nothingness, of neither-ideation-nor-non-ideation (the summit of existence, 有顶).

断非断：见所断、修所断、非所断。

Abandoned and not abandoned: abandoned by seeing, abandoned by cultivation, not to be abandoned.

见所断：见苦、集、灭、道所断。

Abandoned by seeing: abandoned by seeing suffering, origin, cessation and the path.

3.1六因

六因：能作、俱有、同类、相应、遍行、异熟。

The six causes (六因): efficient (能作), co-existent (俱有), homogeneous (同类), associated (相应), universal (遍行), retribution (异熟).

能作因：有为法以除自体之外的一切法为能作因。

Efficient cause: every conditioned dharma has as its efficient cause all dharmas other than itself.

无为法也是能作因；在甲法之后的一切法，也都是甲法的能作因；但甲法并不是无为法的果，也不是其后法的果。无为法不可说前后，未来法之间不可说前后。

Unconditioned dharmas are also efficient causes; all dharmas later than dharma A are also efficient causes of A; but A is neither the fruit of unconditioned dharmas nor the fruit of the dharmas that come after it. Unconditioned dharmas cannot be said to be before or after, nor can future dharmas be said to be before or after one another.

因不一定有果，果不一定有因。能作因不一定有果。离系果没有因。

A cause need not have a fruit, and a fruit need not have a cause. An efficient cause need not have a fruit. The disconnection fruit has no cause.

俱有因：同生一果的诸法互为俱有因；心、心所、二律仪、彼诸相，互为俱有因。

Co-existent cause: dharmas that together produce one fruit are mutually co-existent causes; mind, mental factors, the two restraints [of dhyāna and of the path], and their characteristics are mutually co-existent causes.

同类因：对有漏法来说，自部地前生诸法，是后生相似诸法的同类因；对道谛来说，九地道与后生等胜道为同类因。

Homogeneous cause: for with-outflow dharmas, earlier dharmas of the same class and stage are homogeneous causes of later similar dharmas; for the truth of the path, the path of the nine stages is a homogeneous cause of later paths that are equal or superior.

相应因：同依心心所，互为相应因。

Associated cause: minds and mental factors sharing the same support are mutually associated causes.

遍行因：前生遍行诸法与同地后生染污诸法为遍行因。

Universal cause: earlier universal dharmas are universal causes of later defiled dharmas of the same stage.

异熟因：有漏善及不善，与其异熟果为异熟因。

Retribution cause: with-outflow wholesome and unwholesome dharmas are retribution causes of their retribution fruits.

离系果：择灭。

Disconnection fruit: cessation through deliberation.

异熟果：异熟因所生果。

Retribution fruit: the fruit produced by a retribution cause.

Dominant fruit: the fruit produced by an efficient cause.

能作因未必有果，若有果，是增上果。

An efficient cause need not have a fruit; if it has one, it is a dominant fruit.

等流果：同类、遍行因所生果。

Uniform fruit: the fruit produced by homogeneous and universal causes.

士用果：俱有、相应因所生果。

Virile fruit: the fruit produced by co-existent and associated causes.

等无间缘所生果是士用果（也容是等流果、增上果）。因为等无间缘是俱有、相应因。士用果有四种：俱生、无间、隔越、不生。俱有相应因互为俱生士用果，等无间缘所生是无间士用果，隔越士用果是展转为因力所生起，不生士用果是择灭。

Virile fruits are of four kinds: co-arisen, immediate, separated, and non-arising. Co-existent and associated causes are mutually co-arisen virile fruits; what is produced by an immediately contiguous condition is an immediate virile fruit; a separated virile fruit is produced through a chain of causal force; the non-arising virile fruit is cessation through deliberation.

The ten wholesome courses of action (十善业道): three bodily, four vocal, three mental.

意三恶业道：贪、瞋、邪见。

The three mental unwholesome courses: covetousness (贪), malice (瞋), false view (邪见).

要注意慧、无痴、正见的广狭。慧通善染，无痴唯善；无痴通见非见，正见唯见。尽无生智非见故，非善业道。

Note the scope of understanding, non-delusion and right view. Understanding covers wholesome and defiled; non-delusion is only wholesome. Non-returners going to the fine-material realm: attaining parinirvāṇa in the intermediate state, upon rebirth, with effort, without effort, and going upstream.

行色界者可由业、惑、根分为九种：中般、生般、上流各有速、非速、经久者。上流容行无色。

Those going to the fine-material realm can be divided by karma, defilement and faculties into nine: those attaining parinirvāṇa in the intermediate state, upon rebirth, and going upstream, each being quick, not quick, or long-delayed. One going upstream may go to the immaterial realm.

七善士趣：中般三、生般三、上流。

The seven destinies of good persons (七善士趣): the three of the intermediate state, the three upon rebirth, and the one going upstream.

杂修静虑五品：下、中、上、上胜、上极。如其次第感五净居。已成杂修静虑亦容进离色染生无色界。杂修静虑先杂修第四静虑，次杂修第三二初静虑，以连续三刹那无漏、有漏、无漏为成。五品依次成就三、六、九、十二、十五刹那。

Mixed cultivation of dhyāna (杂修静虑) has five grades: lower, middle, higher, higher-superior, highest. In order they bring about rebirth in the five pure abodes. One who has completed mixed cultivation may also progress to detachment from the fine-material realm and be reborn in the immaterial realm. Mixed cultivation first mixes the fourth dhyāna, then the third, second and first; it is completed by three consecutive moments: outflow-free, with-outflow, outflow-free. The five grades are completed in three, six, nine, twelve and fifteen moments respectively.

得灭定不还名身证。

A non-returner who obtains the attainment of cessation is called a bodily witness (身证).

5.5练根

阿罗汉：退法、思法、护法、安住、堪达、不动。

Arhats: liable to fall (退法), who may end life (思法), who guards (护法), stable (安住), capable of penetrating (堪达), unshakable (不动).

前五从信解生，名时解脱；不动从见至生，名不时解脱。不动亦可由前五练根而得。有学异生亦各六种。中四种容退种性，前五种容退果，虽俱有退然并非先。

The first five arise from those resolved through faith and are called "liberated on occasion" (时解脱); the unshakable arises from those attained through view and is called "liberated not dependent on occasion" (不时解脱). The unshakable may also be reached from the first five by refining the faculties. Trainees and ordinary beings likewise each have six kinds. The middle four may fall from their lineage; the first five may fall from the fruit; although both can fall, it is 'not from the first' (非先).

退：已得退、未得退、受用退。

Falling: from what has been attained, from what has not yet been attained, from enjoyment.

佛唯有受用退。利根（余不动法）容有未得、受用退。钝根容有三退。一切从果退者，命终前必还得先所退果。

The Buddha has only the fall from enjoyment. The sharp-facultied (the unshakable) may have the fall from what has not been attained and from enjoyment. The dull-facultied may have all three. Anyone who falls from a fruit always regains the fruit fallen from before death.

无学练根，九无间九解脱道，依九地；有学练根，一无间一解脱道，依六地。所舍容是果及胜果道，所得唯果道。

The refinement of faculties of one beyond training uses nine uninterrupted and nine liberation paths, based on nine stages; that of a trainee uses one uninterrupted and one liberation path, based on six stages. What is relinquished may be the fruit and the path of a superior fruit; what is obtained is only the fruit-path.

无学圣者有九：退法等五，不动分二（后不动、先不动），独觉，大觉。

There are nine kinds of noble ones beyond training: the five beginning with the one liable to fall; the unshakable divided in two (unshakable later and unshakable from the start); the pratyekabuddha; the Buddha.

七声闻：随信行、随法行、信解、见至、身证、慧解脱、俱解脱。依加行、根、灭定、解脱立。事别唯六：见道、修道、无学道各二种。俱解脱由得灭定。

The seven disciples: faith-follower, dharma-follower, resolved through faith, attained through view, bodily witness, liberated by understanding, liberated both ways. They are distinguished by preparation, faculties, the attainment of cessation, and liberation. As entities they are only six: two each on the paths of seeing, cultivation, and beyond training. One is liberated both ways through obtaining the attainment of cessation.

有学由根、果、定名满；无学由根、定名满。

A trainee is "complete" by faculties, fruit and attainment; one beyond training by faculties and attainment.

四道：加行道、无间道、解脱道、胜进道。

The four paths: preparatory path (加行道), uninterrupted path (无间道), path of liberation (解脱道), path of advancement (胜进道).

四通行：苦迟、苦速、乐迟、乐速。

The four modes of progress: painful and slow, painful and quick, pleasant and slow, pleasant and quick.

依根本四静虑名乐，依余名苦；钝根名迟，利根名速。

Those based on the four fundamental dhyānas are called pleasant; those based on the others, painful. The dull-facultied are called slow, the sharp-facultied quick.

第6章Chapter 6

6.1三十七觉分

三十七觉分：四念住、四正断、四神足、五根、五力、七等觉支、八圣道支。

The thirty-seven factors of awakening (三十七觉分): the four applications of mindfulness, the four right efforts, the four bases of magical power, the five faculties, the five powers, the seven limbs of awakening, the eight limbs of the noble path.

四念住：身、受、心、法。

The four applications of mindfulness: body, feelings, mind, dharmas.

四正断：令已生恶断，令未生恶不生，令未生善生，令已生善增广。

The four right efforts: to abandon evil already arisen, to prevent evil not yet arisen, to produce good not yet arisen, to increase good already arisen.

四神足：欲、勤、心、观。

The four bases of magical power: desire, vigour, mind, investigation.

五根：信、勤（精进）、念、定、慧。

The five faculties: faith, vigour (精进), mindfulness, concentration, understanding.

五力：信、勤、念、定、慧。

The five powers: faith, vigour, mindfulness, concentration, understanding.

七等觉支：念、择法、精进、喜、轻安、定、舍。

The seven limbs of awakening: mindfulness, investigation of dharmas, vigour, joy, pliancy, concentration, equanimity.

八圣道支：正见、正思惟、正语、正业、正命、正精进、正念、正定。

The eight limbs of the noble path: right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right concentration.

此实事唯十：慧、勤、定、信、念、喜、舍、轻安、戒、寻。

As real entities these are only ten: understanding, vigour, concentration, faith, mindfulness, joy, equanimity, pliancy, discipline, vitarka.

慧：四念住、慧根、慧力、择法觉支、正见。

Understanding: the four applications of mindfulness, the faculty of understanding, the power of understanding, the limb of investigation of dharmas, right view.

勤：四正断、精进根、精进力、精进觉支、正精进。

Vigour: the four right efforts, the faculty of vigour, the power of vigour, the limb of vigour, right effort.

定：四神足、定根、定力、定觉支、正定。

Concentration: the four bases of magical power, the faculty of concentration, the power of concentration, the limb of concentration, right concentration.

信：信根、信力。

Faith: the faculty of faith, the power of faith.

念：念根、念力、念觉支、正念。

Mindfulness: the faculty of mindfulness, the power of mindfulness, the limb of mindfulness, right mindfulness.

喜：喜觉支。

Joy: the limb of joy.

行舍：舍觉支。

Equanimity [as a formation]: the limb of equanimity.

轻安：轻安觉支。

Pliancy: the limb of pliancy.

戒：正语、正业、正命。

Discipline: right speech, right action, right livelihood.

寻：正思惟。

Vitarka: right intention.

四念住是慧。若戒分为二，则正语、正命一分是语业，正业、正命一分是身业。七觉八道支唯无漏，余通有漏无漏。

The four applications of mindfulness are understanding. If discipline is divided in two, right speech and one part of right livelihood are vocal action, while right action and the other part of right livelihood are bodily action. The seven limbs of awakening and the eight limbs of the path are only outflow-free; the rest may be with-outflow or outflow-free.

初静虑具三十七支；未至定除喜；第二静虑除寻；第三四静虑及静虑中间除喜、寻；前三无色地除戒、喜、寻；欲界、有顶除七觉、八道支。

The first dhyāna has all thirty-seven; the not-yet-arrived concentration lacks joy; the second dhyāna lacks vitarka; the third and fourth dhyānas and the intermediate dhyāna lack joy and vitarka; the first three immaterial stages lack discipline, joy and vitarka; the sensuous realm and the summit of existence lack the seven limbs of awakening and the eight limbs of the path.

6.2四证净

四证净：佛、法、僧、戒。前三证净以信为体，圣戒证净以戒为体。

The four kinds of faith born of realization (四证净): in the Buddha, the Dharma, the Saṅgha, and in discipline. The first three have faith as substance; the noble discipline has discipline as substance.

法：总法、别法。总法即四谛；别法即苦谛、集谛、灭谛、菩萨道、独觉道。

Dharma: dharma in general and dharma in particular. Dharma in general is the four truths; dharma in particular is the truths of suffering, origin and cessation, the bodhisattva path and the pratyekabuddha path.

佛：佛道（成佛诸无学法）。

Buddha: the Buddha path (the dharmas beyond training that make a Buddha).

僧：僧道（成声闻僧学无学法）。

Saṅgha: the Saṅgha path (the dharmas of trainees and those beyond training that make the disciples' Saṅgha).

戒：无漏戒。

Discipline: outflow-free discipline.

6.3无学十支

无学十支：八圣道、正解脱、正智。

The ten factors of one beyond training (无学十支): the noble eightfold path, right liberation, right knowledge.

解脱：有为解脱（无学胜解）、无为解脱（一切惑灭）。有为解脱名无学支。

Liberation: conditioned liberation (the resolve of one beyond training) and unconditioned liberation (the cessation of all defilements). Conditioned liberation is called a factor of one beyond training.

无为三界：离界、断界、灭界。离界离贪，断界断余结，灭界灭所余贪等随眠所随增事。

The three unconditioned elements: the element of detachment (离界), of abandonment (断界), of cessation (灭界). The element of detachment is detachment from greed; of abandonment, abandonment of the other fetters; of cessation, cessation of the remaining things in which greed and the other anuśayas adhere-and-grow.

厌：缘苦集所起忍智。

Disgust (厌): the receptivities and knowledges arising with suffering and origin as object.

离：缘四谛境所起忍智能断惑者。

Detachment (离): the receptivities and knowledges arising with the four truths as object that are able to abandon defilements.

6.4三界诸处

欲界二十处：八地狱、傍生、鬼、人四洲、六欲天。

The twenty places of the sensuous realm: eight hells, animals, ghosts, the four continents of humans, the six heavens of the sensuous realm.

八地狱：等活、黑绳、众合、号叫、大叫、炎热、大热、无间。

The eight hells: Saṃjīva, Kālasūtra, Saṃghāta, Raurava, Mahāraurava, Tapana, Pratāpana, Avīci.

人四洲：南赡部、东胜身、西牛货、北俱卢。

The four continents of humans: Jambudvīpa in the south, Pūrvavideha in the east, Avaragodānīya in the west, Uttarakuru in the north.

六欲天：四大王众天、三十三天、夜摩天、覩史多天、乐变化天、他化自在天。

The six heavens of the sensuous realm: the heaven of the Four Great Kings, the Thirty-three, Yāma, Tuṣita, Nirmāṇarati, Paranirmitavaśavartin.

色界十七处：初静虑三、第二静虑三、第三静虑三、第四静虑八。

The seventeen places of the fine-material realm: three of the first dhyāna, three of the second, three of the third, eight of the fourth.

初静虑三：梵众天、梵辅天、大梵天。有部认为色界唯十六处，大梵王所居即梵辅处。

The three of the first dhyāna: Brahmakāyika, Brahmapurohita, Mahābrahman. The Sarvāstivāda holds that the fine-material realm has only sixteen places, the abode of the Great Brahmā being within the place of Brahmapurohita.

第二静虑三：少光天、无量光天、极光净天。

The three of the second dhyāna: Parīttābha, Apramāṇābha, Ābhāsvara.

第三静虑三：少净天、无量净天、遍净天。

The three of the third dhyāna: Parīttaśubha, Apramāṇaśubha, Śubhakṛtsna.

第四静虑八：无云天、福生天、广果天、无烦天、无热天、善现天、善见天、色究竟天。无想有情居广果天。

The eight of the fourth dhyāna: Anabhraka, Puṇyaprasava, Bṛhatphala, Avṛha, Atapa, Sudṛśa, Sudarśana, Akaniṣṭha. Beings without ideation dwell in Bṛhatphala.

无色界无处，由异熟生差别立空无边等四处。

The immaterial realm has no places; the four [spheres] of infinite space etc. are established by differences in the arising of retribution.

五趣：地狱、傍生、鬼、人、天。

The five destinies (五趣): hell, animals, ghosts, humans, gods.

四生：卵生、胎生、湿生、化生。

The four modes of birth (四生): from an egg, from a womb, from moisture, apparitional.

人、傍生具四生。地狱、诸天、中有唯化生。鬼通胎生化生。

Humans and animals have all four modes of birth. Hell beings, gods and intermediate beings are only apparitional. Ghosts are born from a womb or apparitionally.

七识住：身异想异、身异想一、身一想异、身一想一、无色下三。

The seven stations of consciousness (七识住): diverse bodies and diverse ideations; diverse bodies and uniform ideation; uniform bodies and diverse ideations; uniform bodies and uniform ideation; and the lower three immaterial [spheres].

九有情居：七识住、有顶、无想有情。

The nine abodes of beings (九有情居): the seven stations of consciousness, the summit of existence, and beings without ideation.

6.5缘起十二支

缘起十二支：前际二、后际二、中际八。

The twelve links of dependent origination (缘起十二支): two of the prior period, two of the later period, eight of the middle period.

前际二：无明、行。

The two of the prior period: ignorance, formations.

中际八：识、名色、六处、触、受、爱、取、有。

The eight of the middle period: consciousness, name-and-form, the six sense-bases, contact, feeling, craving, grasping, existence.

后际二：生、老死。

The two of the later period: birth, aging-and-death.

三烦恼：无明、爱、取。

The three defilements: ignorance, craving, grasping.

二业：行、有。

The two actions: formations, existence.

七事（果）：识、名色、六处、触、受；生、老死。

The seven things (fruits): consciousness, name-and-form, the six sense-bases, contact, feeling; birth, aging-and-death.

名：受、想、行、识。

Name: feeling, ideation, formations, consciousness.

六触：眼触、耳触、鼻触、舌触、身触、意触。前五是有对触，意触是增语触。

The six contacts: eye-, ear-, nose-, tongue-, body-, mind-contact. The first five are contacts with resistance (有对触); mind-contact is designation contact (增语触).

十八意近行：喜六近行、忧六近行、舍六近行。皆意相应，六近行缘色等六境，唯有漏。

The eighteen mental approaches (十八意近行): six approaches of joy, six of displeasure, six of equanimity. All are associated with mind [-consciousness]; the six approaches take visible form and the other five objects as object; they are only with-outflow.

6.6世间

四食：段、触、思、识。

The four foods (四食): morsel food, contact, volition, consciousness.

断善根、续善根、离染、退、死、生，唯许意识。死生唯许舍受相应，唯散非定，唯有心位。

Cutting off the wholesome roots, reconnecting them, detachment, regression, death and birth are allowed only in mind-consciousness. Death and birth are allowed only in association with the feeling of equanimity, only in a non-meditative state, not a meditative one, and only in a state with mind.

四劫：成劫、住劫、坏劫、空劫。

The four eons: of formation, duration, destruction and emptiness.

成住坏空各二十中劫，总此八十成大劫量。劫性唯五蕴。

Formation, duration, destruction and emptiness each last twenty intermediate eons; these eighty make up one great eon. The nature of an eon is only the five aggregates.

三灾：火灾、水灾、风灾。

The three calamities: fire, water, wind.

千四大洲乃至梵世，名一小千界。千倍小千名一中千界。千中千界总名一大千。大千同成同坏。大千唯至梵世。

A thousand four-continent worlds, up to the Brahmā world, are called a small chiliocosm. A thousand small chiliocosms are a middle chiliocosm. A thousand middle chiliocosms together are called a great chiliocosm. A great chiliocosm is formed and destroyed together. A great chiliocosm reaches only up to the Brahmā world.

6.7罪福

三障：业障、烦恼障、异熟障。

The three obstructions (三障): of action, of defilement, of retribution.

业障唯五无间业，烦恼障唯数行烦恼，异熟障是全三恶趣、人趣北洲、无想天。

The obstruction of action is only the five deeds of immediate retribution; the obstruction of defilement is only habitually active defilements; the obstruction of retribution is all three evil destinies, the northern continent among humans, and the gods without ideation.

五无间业：害母、害父、害阿罗汉、破和合僧、恶心出佛身血。

The five deeds of immediate retribution (五无间业): killing one's mother, killing one's father, killing an arhat, splitting the harmonious Saṅgha, drawing the Buddha's blood with evil intent.

六波罗蜜多：施、戒、忍、进、定、慧。

The six perfections (六波罗蜜多): giving, discipline, patience, vigour, meditation, understanding.

三福业事：施、戒、修。

The three bases of meritorious action (三福业事): giving, discipline, cultivation.

顺三分善：顺福、顺解脱、顺决择。如次感爱果、涅槃、圣道。

Wholesome [roots] conducive to three portions: to merit, to liberation, to penetration. They bring about, respectively, desirable fruits, nirvāṇa, and the noble path.

善无漏法名妙；染有罪法名覆、劣；善有为法名应习；解脱涅槃名无上。

Wholesome outflow-free dharmas are called excellent (妙); defiled, blameworthy dharmas are called concealed (覆) and inferior (劣); wholesome conditioned dharmas are called to-be-cultivated (应习); liberation, nirvāṇa, is called unsurpassed (无上).

在线工具Online tools

CBETA Online
中华电子佛典。毘昙部原文检索，作者引文均出于此。The Chinese Buddhist canon online; search the Abhidharma texts here.
SAT
大正藏数据库，可检索快道林常《俱舍论法义》等。The Taishō database; needed for Kaidō's Hōgi.
DDB
Digital Dictionary of Buddhism。作者曾就阿毗达磨词条向主编穆勒教授反馈，多数获采纳。Digital Dictionary of Buddhism; the author's corrections to Abhidharma entries were mostly adopted by its editor, Charles Muller.
GRETIL
梵文电子文本库。《英译百瑕》摘录了其中部分梵本，作者提醒录文间有错误。Sanskrit e-texts; the Flaws quotes some of them, with the author's caution that the transcriptions contain errors.

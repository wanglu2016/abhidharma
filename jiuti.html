入门测验Entrance test

作者在序中给出的两道题：Two questions from the preface:

  1. 默写出九十八随眠、七十五法、三十七道品、二十二根。Write out from memory the 98 anuśayas, the 75 dharmas, the 37 factors of awakening and the 22 faculties.
  2. 能解释二十二根「是异熟」、「非异熟」、「有异熟」、「无异熟」、「三性」、「何所断」等情况。Explain, for the 22 faculties, which are retribution, not retribution, having retribution, without retribution, their three natures, and by what they are abandoned.

如果能在半小时内闭卷完全正确地答出上面两题，即具备了阅读本书的基础。如果暂时不具备，也没有关系，可以一边阅读本书前四题，一边背《俱舍论颂》，齐头并进。If you can answer both completely and correctly, closed-book, in half an hour, you are ready for the book. If not yet, that is fine: read the first four questions while memorising the Verses, in parallel.

怎样拆分问题How to split a question

三界阿羅漢是否成就三世無漏色？
Do arhats of the three realms possess outflow-free matter of the three times?

有人以為答案無外乎「是」或「否」，就想來蒙，這怎麼可能呢？「三界阿羅漢」指的是：欲界阿羅漢、色界阿羅漢、無色界阿羅漢；「三世」指的是：過去、現在、未來。……問題共有九個部分。而且，九個部分中，並不是每一個部分都可以直接用是或否來回答。比如，欲界阿羅漢是否成就現在無漏色？要看欲界阿羅漢是否住無漏定中。Some think the answer must be yes or no and try to guess. 'Arhats of the three realms' means arhats of the sensuous, fine-material and immaterial realms; 'the three times' means past, present, future. The question has nine parts, and not every part can be answered yes or no: whether a sensuous-realm arhat possesses present outflow-free matter depends on whether he is abiding in outflow-free meditation.《俱舍九题》第 17 页Nine Questions, p. 17
登堂Entering the hall

前四题总体上比较简单，尤其是前两题，是为了照顾基础不牢的读者入门的。不过，第一题第四小节以后稍有难度，可先跳过。三四题相比前两题难度稍涨。能搞清楚前四题，阿毗达磨学习可以说登堂了。建议在学习前四题的同时，把《俱舍论颂》背熟。The first four are fairly easy overall, especially the first two, which are there to bring in readers whose foundation is weak (from section 4 of Q1 it gets harder and can be skipped at first). Q3 and Q4 are a little harder. Master these four and you have 'entered the hall'. Memorise the Verses while you study them.

1《俱舍》的门槛The threshold

諸業以諸法為何果？

For each kind of action, which of the five fruits are the various kinds of dharma?

Runs the five fruits and six causes through every classification: wholesome/unwholesome/indeterminate, the three times, the stages, trainee/beyond training, modes of abandonment. Ends with 'how many have self-view as cause but are not its cause' and the notions of co-existent dharmas and dharmas that follow the mind.

  • 一、善等業以善等法為哪些果？p. 22
  • 二、三界十五部、遍行品p. 28
  • 三、餘問p. 31
  • 四、「幾有身見為因非有身見因等」p. 34
  • 五、「俱有法」、「心隨轉法」、「隨行」p. 41

相关颂Verse：业品 90　善等於善等，初有四二三，中有二三四，後二三三果。

2《俱舍》的地基The foundation

業在蘊處界中是何所攝？

In which aggregates, sense-bases and elements is action included?

Locates mental, bodily and vocal action in the aggregates, bases and elements; then asks by what each wholesome and unwholesome course of action is abandoned, and separates non-delusion from right view.

  • 一、意業在蘊處界中是何所攝？p. 47
  • 二、身語業在蘊處界中是何所攝？p. 49
  • 三、諸善業道、不善業道，分別是何所斷？p. 53
  • 四、「無痴」與「正見」p. 57

3《俱舍》的大肥肉The meatiest part

十二心無間相生

The immediate succession of the twelve minds

When a mind arises, which minds can follow it in the next moment, and which can have preceded it? Worked out for the sensuous realm and then the higher realms; ends with 'how many are wholesome but not causes of the wholesome'.

  • 一、欲界善心，無間能生幾心？p. 61
  • 二、欲界善心，容由幾心無間而生？p. 67
  • 三、欲界餘心p. 68
  • 四、上界諸心p. 70
  • 五、「幾善非善為因等」p. 76

相关颂Verse：根品 68　欲界善生九，此復從八生，染從十生四，餘從五生七。

4《俱舍》的瑕疵The flaw

十二心中何心現前幾心可得？

Among the twelve minds, when a given mind is manifest, how many minds are acquired?

The acquisition of minds when defiled minds of the three realms, or wholesome minds of the fine-material realm, become manifest; closes with the verse beginning 'the wise say that the defiled mind…', where the author locates a flaw in the Kośa.

  • 一、三界染心現前，幾心可得？p. 81
  • 二、色界善心現前，幾心可得？p. 87
  • 三、餘問p. 90
  • 四、「慧者說染心」一頌p. 92

相关颂Verse：根品 74　三界染心中，得六六二種，色善三學四，餘皆自可得。

能搞明白五、六题，阿毗达磨就入室了。搞懂第七题，就是阿毗达磨专家了。Understand Q5 and Q6 and you have 'entered the inner room'. Understand Q7 and you are an Abhidharma expert.

5《俱舍》的中流Midstream

五受根何時創得何時捨？

When are the five feeling faculties first acquired, and when relinquished?

Pain and mental displeasure; pleasure; joy and equanimity: their first acquisition and relinquishment, outflow-free ones included.

6《俱舍》的承重墙The load-bearing wall

五受根相應煩惱，約九十八隨眠、約七十五法，分別是何所斷？

The defilements associated with each of the five feeling faculties: counted as the 98 anuśayas and as the 75 dharmas, by what is each abandoned?

Taken faculty by faculty (equanimity, pain, displeasure, joy, pleasure), this requires the full grid of the 98 anuśayas and the mental factors at once.

7《俱舍》的司令部Headquarters

十五部隨眠中，哪些於五上分結緣緣識隨增？

Of the fifteen classes of anuśaya, which adhere-and-grow in the consciousness that cognizes [a consciousness] cognizing the five higher fetters?

The hardest machinery of the anuśaya chapter: second-order objects (緣緣識) for the higher and lower fetters and the twenty-two faculties; 'cause, object, abandonment, consciousness'; and the patterns called 一行、歷六、小七、大七.

8《俱舍》的大海The ocean

何時修無漏初靜慮非淨？

When does one cultivate the outflow-free first dhyāna but not the pure one?

From the Jñānaprasthāna (fasc. 17), answered at length in the Vibhāṣā (fasc. 163–164): the four-way structure of cultivating the pure and outflow-free dhyāna, through direct realization, detachment, refinement of faculties, mixed cultivation and other qualities; ending with 'acquisition before the dharma', 'future acquisition', and kulaṃkula and samāhita.

9《俱舍》的天际The horizon

定生善法等

Wholesome dharmas born of meditation, and other questions

Difficult details, some still open: the two senses of 'aids to penetration'; relinquishing wholesome dharmas born of meditation and the superior roots; 'change of stage' versus 'crossing stages'; reduction of aspects and objects in middle receptivity; detachment in the intermediate state; 'five fall from the fruit, not first'; whether the first dhyāna path based on the second is a homogeneous cause; and the acquisition of disconnection.

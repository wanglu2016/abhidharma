说一切有部阿毗达磨学习Studying Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma

以《阿毗达磨俱舍论》为中心，按王路的四部著作安排学习：先看全局地图，再读纲要、背颂文，最后进入《俱舍九题》的高阶问题。Centred on the Abhidharmakośa and organised around Wang Lu's four books: see the whole map first, then read the outline and learn the verses, and finally take on the advanced problems of the Nine Questions.

学习路线A route through the material

下面的次序整理自作者在《俱舍九题》序与要点中的建议。This sequence follows the author's advice in the preface and key points of the Nine Questions.

  1. 读纲要，记住“是什么”Read the outline: remember the 'what'

    《俱舍万字》用约一万字列出《俱舍》纲目。作者说基础阶段“不适合多问为什么，而适合多记住是什么”；前面看不懂的先跳过。The Ten Thousand Characters lays out the Kośa's framework in about ten thousand characters. At this stage, the author says, remember what things are rather than asking why; skip what you don't yet understand.

  2. 背《俱舍论颂》Memorise the Verses

    作者心目中熟练的标准：八十分钟内背完全部颂，随机抽取任意一颂都能正确讲解。颂文页提供随机抽颂和遮盖自测。The author's benchmark: recite all the verses within eighty minutes and correctly explain any verse drawn at random. The verse page offers random draws and masking for self-testing.

  3. 按建议次序通读《俱舍》，对照地图Read the Kośa in the suggested order, with the map

    建议次序：定品；贤圣品、随眠品；界品、根品；业品；世间品、智品。地图标出每个概念属于哪一品、作者认为它是要点、难点还是基本操作。Suggested order: Meditation; Path and Anuśaya; Elements and Faculties; Action; World and Knowledge. The map shows which chapter each concept belongs to and whether the author treats it as a key point, a difficulty or a basic operation.

  4. 做入门测验Take the entrance test

    默写九十八随眠、七十五法、三十七道品、二十二根；能解释二十二根的异熟、三性、何所断等。半小时内闭卷全对，即具备读《九题》的基础。Write out the 98 anuśayas, 75 dharmas, 37 factors of awakening and 22 faculties; explain the faculties by retribution, nature and mode of abandonment. All correct, closed-book, in half an hour means you are ready for the Nine Questions.

  5. 读《俱舍九题》Work through the Nine Questions

    前四题“登堂”，五、六题“入室”，第七题“专家”，八、九题是向阿毗达磨深处的探寻。全日制、每日五小时，从零起步约八个月。Questions 1–4: entering the hall; 5–6: the inner room; 7: expert; 8–9: the far reaches. Full-time, five hours a day, about eight months from zero.

作者谈方法The author on method

要学好阿毗达磨，必须反反复复看《婆沙》、《俱舍》、《正理》原文，有些段落可能要读过五十遍以上。不过要记住，不是让你通读……所有读过五十遍以上的段落，基本上都是因为碰到具体问题去检索，然后反复读。To learn Abhidharma well you must read the Vibhāṣā, the Kośa and the Nyāyānusāra again and again; some passages fifty times or more. But not by reading straight through: those passages get read fifty times because a concrete problem sends you searching for them.《俱舍九题》序
我强烈建议结合问题学习《俱舍》。I strongly recommend studying the Kośa through problems.《俱舍九题》要点
可以尝试一个概念一个概念地学。但千万不要企图“掌握了一个概念之后再学下一个”——不把所有概念学完，有些概念是无论如何也掌握不了的。You can try to learn concept by concept, but never try to master one before moving to the next: some concepts cannot be grasped until all have been learned.《俱舍九题》要点
阿毗达磨的核心就是两个字：离染。The core of Abhidharma is one word: detachment.《俱舍九题》要点

四部著作Four books

难度按作者自己的说明排列。Ordered by difficulty, as the author describes them.

《俱舍万字》Ten Thousand Characters on the Kośa

基础Basic

《俱舍》纲目，约一万字，用简体写成，面向初学。本站全文收录并配英译。The Kośa's framework in ~10,000 characters, for beginners. Full text with English on this site.

《俱舍英译百瑕》One Hundred Flaws in the English Kośa

比《九题》简单Easier than the Nine Questions

逐条指出 Pruden 与 Lodrö Sangpo 两部英译本的一百处错误。附录有十余条英文稿。One hundred errors in the Pruden and Lodrö Sangpo translations, entry by entry; an appendix gives a dozen in English.

《阿毗达磨理校百例》One Hundred Doctrinal Emendations

难于《百瑕》，易于《九题》Harder than the Flaws, easier than the Nine Questions

依阿毗达磨义理校正《大正藏》毘昙部的误字，示范如何“理校”。Corrects scribal errors in the Taishō Abhidharma section by doctrinal reasoning, showing how such emendation works.

《俱舍九题》The Nine Questions of the Kośa

高阶Advanced

高阶阿毗达磨串讲。九个问题贯穿全论，2024 年在台湾出版。An advanced course: nine problems that run through the whole treatise. Published in Taiwan, 2024.

给中文不熟练的读者If Chinese is not your first language

作者认为玄奘的译语最为精确：“只懂汉语完全可以”学好《俱舍》，而只依靠英译本则会被大量误译带偏。本站为英文读者准备了：The author holds that Xuanzang's Chinese is the most precise vehicle for the Kośa, and that relying on the English translations alone leads readers astray at many points. For English readers this site provides:

  • 《俱舍万字》全文逐段英译，以及近百个概念的中、梵、英对照释义；an English rendering of the whole outline, and some ninety concepts with Chinese, Sanskrit and English;
  • 《英译百瑕》一百条的英文提要，标出颂号和两部英译本的页码，可以边读英译边查；English summaries of all one hundred Translation Flaws, keyed to verse numbers and to page numbers in Pruden and Lodrö Sangpo, for use while reading;
  • 界面可切换为中英对照：中文术语始终保留，便于在 CBETA 检索原文。a parallel Chinese–English view; Chinese terms are always kept so you can search the originals on CBETA.

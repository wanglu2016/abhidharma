《阿毗达磨理校百例》索引One Hundred Doctrinal Emendations: index

依据阿毗达磨义理，对《大正藏》毘昙部汉译论典（《集异门》T1536 至《显宗》T1563，《发智》居首）进行“理校”：判断异文中何者为是，或指出诸本皆误之处。讨论对象是截至 2024 年 6 月 30 日 CBETA 尚未修订的错误。作者说：“当你懂得一例，就能看懂三例；当你懂得一百例，就几乎可以看懂所有可以运用阿毗达磨来理校的地方。”Emendation by doctrinal reasoning (理校) of the Chinese Abhidharma treatises in the Taishō (T1536–T1563, with the Jñānaprasthāna first): deciding between variant readings, or showing that all witnesses are wrong. It covers errors not yet corrected in CBETA as of 30 June 2024. 'Understand one example and you can follow three; understand a hundred and you can follow almost every place where Abhidharma can be used to emend a text.'

难度：比《英译百瑕》难一些，比《俱舍九题》简单一些。下表列出每例所在论典、卷次、CBETA 位置和所讨论文本的开头；点页码打开 PDF。Difficulty: a little harder than the Translation Flaws, a little easier than the Nine Questions. Each line gives the text, fascicle, CBETA locus and the opening of the passage discussed; click the page number to open the PDF.
